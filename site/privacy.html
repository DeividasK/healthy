Healthy

Privacy Policy

Healthy · Effective 26 October 2026

Healthy is a personal health record app for the web, Android and iOS. It is built so that your health information stays under your control. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and who can read it.

Summary

  • The developer never receives or stores your health records. On the web, the developer's sign-in helper passes your Google sign-in tokens on to Google without keeping them.
  • There are no accounts on our side, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only server is a small Google sign-in helper that never sees your health data and stores nothing.
  • Your records are stored on your device.
  • Optional Google Drive sync stores your records in your own Google Drive, end-to-end encrypted, so neither Google nor the developer can read them.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter (profiles, conditions, consultations, treatments, lab results and related notes) is saved in a database on your device. On the web, it is saved in your browser's storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it or turn on sync.

Exports (for example a .zip backup) are files you create and share yourself. Where they go is up to you.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you connect a Google account to sync between your devices:

  • The app asks Google for your basic profile (name, email address and account ID) to show which account is connected, and for access to a hidden app-only folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Your records are encrypted on your device before upload, with a key generated on your device and shown to you as a 24-word recovery phrase. Google stores only encrypted files and can see their names, sizes and timestamps, but not their contents.
  • Your sign-in tokens and encryption key are kept in your device's secure storage (or your browser's storage on the web). Your encryption key never leaves your device.
  • On the web, Google only issues long-lived sign-in tokens to apps that hold a secret key, which cannot ship inside a public web app. So the web app passes your Google sign-in tokens through a small sign-in helper run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers, which adds the key and forwards them to Google. It handles Google tokens only, never your health data, and keeps no copy or logs of them.
  • Google's handling of your account and Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Keep your recovery phrase safe. Without it, nobody (including the developer) can decrypt your synced data.

Data sharing

The developer does not sell, share or transfer your data. Your health records never reach the developer, and the sign-in helper only passes Google sign-in tokens through without keeping them. The app talks only to Google, and on the web to the sign-in helper hosted on Cloudflare, and only when you use Google sign-in and sync.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a profile in the app to remove its records from your device.
  • When deleting a synced profile, you can choose to delete it from Google Drive too. You can also remove the app's data from Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps in Google Drive, and revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the Android or iOS app removes all data it stored on that device. On the web, uninstalling or closing the app keeps its data: clear the site data for usehealthy.app in your browser's settings to remove it.

Children

Healthy is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Healthy is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
