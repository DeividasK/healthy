Everything in one place

Lab results that make sense

200+ common tests with their units and reference ranges, matched to your age and gender. See what's out of range and how it changes over time.

Import with your own AI

Paste your lab report into the AI assistant you already use. Healthy fills in the results for you to check.

Everything connected

Link conditions, doctor visits, treatments and referrals, and follow each condition on its own timeline.

A journal for medications

Log the medications and supplements you take, morning to bedtime, in a tap.

Records for the whole family

Keep a separate profile for each person you care for.

Backed up your way

Export everything to a file, or sync encrypted between devices through your own Google Drive.

Private by design

  • Your records live on your device. Healthy has no server, no ads and no analytics.
  • Sync is optional and encrypted on your device before it reaches your Google Drive. Google can't read it.
  • Built on the medical standards hospitals use (HL7 FHIR, LOINC, UCUM), so your data stays portable.
  • Open source, so anyone can check these claims in the code.

Open source. Built in the open.

Every line of Healthy is on GitHub. Check how your data is handled, fork it, adapt it, or help build it. Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are all welcome.
