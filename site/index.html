Healthy

Your health, understood.

One home for your blood tests, supplements and medical history. See the big picture, spot what needs attention, and know what to bring to your next appointment.

Open Healthy Free, forever. Private, on your device. Works in your browser.

Everything in one view

Every biomarker, one timeline

Lipids, glucose, hormones, inflammation, vitamins and more, including every marker in WHOOP Advanced Labs. See each one change across all your tests.

Ranges that fit you

Reference ranges follow your age and gender, so you see what's really out of range at a glance.

Ratios worked out for you

HOMA-IR, the atherogenic index, cholesterol ratios and more are calculated from each report, so they always match your results.

Import with your own AI

Paste your lab report into the AI assistant you already use. Healthy fills in the results for you to check.

Track your protocol

Log your supplements and medications by time of day, in a tap, and keep a record of every regimen you've tried.

The rest of your health

Conditions, doctor visits and treatments live alongside your results, for you and your family.

Private by design

  • Your records live on your device. Healthy has no server, no ads and no analytics.
  • Sync is optional and encrypted on your device before it reaches your Google Drive. Google can't read it.
  • Built on the medical standards hospitals use (HL7 FHIR, LOINC, UCUM), so your data stays portable.
  • Open source, so anyone can check these claims in the code.
